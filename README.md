# 📚 Blockchain in Supply Chain – Research Summary

This table compiles key academic sources related to blockchain integration in supply chains, focusing on transparency, traceability, and sustainability.

| Source | Year | Research | Author(s) | Methodology | Key Findings | Research Gap |
|--------|------|----------|-----------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| [IEEE Xplore](#) | 2024 | *Blockchain Technology for Transparency in Agri-Food Supply Chain: Use Cases, Limitations, and Future Directions* | Sheetal Menon, Karuna Jain | Thematic Analysis | Identifies 25 use cases enhancing transparency; emphasizes traceability, immutability, auditability | Limited empirical evidence on real-world applications |
| [Taylor and Francis](#) | 2023 | *Blockchain Adoption in Supply Chain Management: A Review* | Nam Vu, Abhijeet Ghadge, Michael Bourlakis | Systematic Literature Review | Highlights benefits but notes lack of empirical proof | Need for case studies to validate theoretical claims |
| [ResearchGate](#) | 2018 | *The Role of Blockchain in Achieving Sustainable Supply Chains* | Sara Saberi, Mahtab Kouhizadeh, Joseph Sarkis, Lejia Shen | Qualitative Analysis | Links blockchain to improved transparency and reduced waste | Insufficient exploration of long-term impacts |
| [ScienceDirect](#) | 2020 | *Blockchain and Sustainable Supply Chain Management in Developing Countries* | Kshetri | Literature Review | Addresses consumer trust and safety via traceability | Limited understanding of consumer perception |
| [ResearchGate](#) | 2020 | *Blockchain Adoption in Logistics and Supply Chain: A Literature Review and Research Agenda* | Serkan Karakaş, A. Zafer Acar, Berk Kucukaltan | Qualitative Research | Focuses on transparency in logistics | Need for quantitative impact measures |
| [ScienceDirect](#) | 2019 | *Leveraging Blockchain for Enhanced Traceability in Food Supply Chains* | Sachin S. Kamble, Angappa Gunasekaran, Rohit Sharma | Empirical Study | Shows blockchain improves traceability and food safety | Needs broader studies across food sectors |
| [ResearchGate](#) | 2023 | *Blockchain in Food Supply Chains: A Survey and Synthesis* | Dr-Deepak Kumar Acharya, Manish Shrivastava | Meta-Analysis | Reviews platforms and benefits like fraud reduction | Lack of clarity on implementation challenges |
| [ScienceDirect](#) | 2021 | *Blockchain Technology and the Sustainable Supply Chain: Exploring Barriers* | Mahtab Kouhizadeh, Sara Saberi, Joseph Sarkis | Theoretical Exploration | - | Lack of practical barrier analysis |
| [Emerald](#) | 2025 | *Blockchain-enabled Sustainable SCM: Impact of Collaboration Optimization* | Yanhong Wu | AMOS SEM + ANN | Blockchain competency boosts collaboration and innovation | Limited longitudinal studies on resilience |
| [ResearchGate](#) | 2022 | *Blockchain Technology for Supply Chain Management: A Comprehensive Review* | Aichih (Jasmine) Chang, Nesreen El-Rayes, Jim Junmin Shi | Systematic Literature Review (2291 papers) | Traceability is key; market to reach $1.4B by 2028 | Lack of empirical framework validation |
| [Link](#) | 2024 | *Exploring Top Blockchain Use Cases in SCM for 2025* | - | Case Study Analysis | 75% adoption rate, 30% cost reduction | Limited focus on regulation & stakeholder resistance |
| [ScienceDirect](#) | 2019 | *Blockchain Adoption Challenges in Supply Chain* | Maciel M. Queiroz, Samuel Fosso Wamba | Empirical Investigation | Performance expectancy drives adoption in India & USA | Early-stage adoption with disruptive potential |
